OUR EDITORIAL FOUNDATION

A sense of wonder. A respect for evidence.

Documented events, personal testimony, religious interpretation, and unexplained claims will be labelled distinctly. Sponsorship will never determine a story’s evidence status.

Every published record should carry a source trail, a review date, an evidence label, and a corrections route. The ambition is worldwide coverage; the catalogue will show its actual verified scope as it grows.
